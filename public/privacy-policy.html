2. Notifications and alarms

If you enable Azan reminders, the app schedules notifications on your device for prayer times. These are created locally; no notification content is sent to us. Android may ask you to allow "alarms & reminders" so reminders arrive on time.

3. Third-party services

  • Quran text, translation and recitation are loaded from api.quran.com and api.alquran.cloud. Those services receive your IP address when you open the Quran section.
  • Fonts may be downloaded from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com).
  • Website pages shown inside the app (Naat, Account, Donate and others) are served by our own website and are covered by this policy.

We do not show advertisements, and we do not sell your personal information.

4. How we use and protect data

We use the information only to run the features described above. Data is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time using the in-app Account → Delete Account button or at /delete-account.html. Details of what is deleted and what we may keep are explained on that page.

6. Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices

  • You can deny or withdraw location, camera, photo and notification permissions at any time in your phone's Settings. The app still works; prayer times then need a manually chosen city.
  • You can log out at any time and request deletion of your data.
  • You can clear on-device data by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do.

Delete Your Markaz-e-Durood Account

This page applies to the Markaz-e-Durood app (com.markazedurood.app) and the website markaz-e-durood.vercel.app.

How to request deletion

  1. Tap the button below. It opens an email addressed to us.
  2. Send it from the email address you registered with, so we can confirm the account is yours. Keep the subject line as it is.
  3. We will confirm by email and delete your account within 30 days.

Request account deletion

If the button does not open your email app, write to mkzedurood@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Markaz-e-Durood account".

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email, password)
  • Your profile information and durood submissions linked to your account

What may be kept

  • Donation records (amount, date, currency), which we may need to keep for accounting and legal reasons. They will be disconnected from your personal details where possible.
  • Anonymous totals, such as the overall community durood count and visitor statistics, which do not identify you.

Data stored only on your phone

Settings, prayer times preferences and your profile picture are stored on your device only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes them.
